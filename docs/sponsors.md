# SideStream Hack 2026: sponsor research

Compiled 22 Oct 2026 for the Technion / Carasso FoodTech Innovation Center hackathon. Every figure below is taken from the linked source. Where a claim was seen only in a search-result snippet (the page itself could not be opened), it is marked *(snippet)*.

**Event facts (from sidestream2026.com JS bundle, Hebrew brief):**
- Tiers: Platinum = Tnuva, NVIDIA, Strauss; Gold = Magnum, CBC Israel; Silver = Tosaf.
- Sponsorship packages: Platinum (NIS 10,000) = up to **3 challenges**, Gold (NIS 7,500) = up to **2**, Silver (NIS 6,000) = up to **1**. The maximum is therefore ~13 challenges.
- Organised by Food Forward, the Carasso Center's food-industry innovation ecosystem, with the Innovation & Industry Relations division and support from the Ministry of Economy and Industry.
- The original target list also named Nestlé-Osem, Unilever, Gan Shmuel, Matzot Aviv, Zoglowek, Bright Food (Tnuva's owner), Green Frost (Milotal), Prodalim, Amber feed-mix institute and Ayelet spices.
- Stated themes: material/biological side streams, chemical waters, CO2, packaging residues, wastewater, energy/emissions. Goal: at least one idea goes to a company test or pilot. There is a NIS 29,000 pilot track at the Carasso Center.
- Mapping file: `sponsor_streams.csv`. New stream rows: `/workspace/sidestream/shards/streams_SPONSOR.csv` (SPONSOR-0001…0011).

## 1. Tnuva (Platinum)

**Who:** Israel's largest food company and dairy cooperative.
- Owners: China's Bright Food (77%) and kibbutz organisations (23%).
- Estimated 2024 revenue: ~NIS 9 bn.
- Exited the regulated egg business in January 2024. [calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/shopping/article/bj6apqgdyx), [calcalist eggs](https://www.calcalist.co.il/shopping/article/bkdz09qka)

**Israeli sites:**
- Dairies in Rehovot, Alon Tavor, Tel Yosef, Harduf, and Tene Noga at Be'er Tuvia / Kiryat Malachi. [newsinisrael](https://newsinisrael.co.uk/on-the-occasion-of-the-milk-festival-the-three-largest-dairies-in-israel-are-opening-their-doors-a-special-project/)
- **Ba'emek Advanced Technologies** whey plant at Alon Tavor (built 2001). It makes WPC 34–80%, whey protein hydrolysates and edible/refined lactose from whey collected nationwide, mainly from Edam and cottage cheese. [Atlantium case study](https://atlantium.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/PMO_Tnuva-Baemek-Case-Study_EN_Digital_010224.pdf)
- Meat: Adom Adom cattle plant in the Beit She'an industrial area, poultry slaughterhouses, and the Tiv Tirat Zvi meat-products plant *(snippet)*. [Stern Group](https://www.stern.co.il/en/projects/commerce-industry/61)
- Plant-based: Tnuva Alternative / Gilboa Alternative.

**Side streams with published numbers:**
- **Whey → COW water.** 75% of the whey volume reaching Ba'emek ends up as "COW water". Tnuva treats the RO permeate with Atlantium HOD UV (since 2012) and reuses it as CIP/rinse water, saving ~USD 162k/yr in energy. [Atlantium](https://atlantium.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/PMO_Tnuva-Baemek-Case-Study_EN_Digital_010224.pdf)
- Ba'emek was designed for **200,000 m3/yr of whey** *(snippet)*. [just-food](https://www.just-food.com/news/israel-tnuva-invests-in-us34m-whey-plant/)
- **Tene Noga pre-treatment:** NIS 20 M membrane concentration unit handling ~25,000 t/yr of whey. The concentrate goes to Ba'emek; the residual water goes to sewer and then to irrigation. Before this, the whey was discharged to the sea. [infospot](http://infospot.co.il/n/tnuva_inroduces_cheese_water_recycling)
- **Salty cheese brine:** Be'er Tuvia discharged salty brine/whey to the sea via Shafdan above permitted limits in 2009–10. The fine was proposed at NIS 15.2 M and set at NIS 7.6 M. [ynetnews](https://www.ynetnews.com/articles/0,7340,L-4178214,00.html), [ashdodnet](https://ashdodnet.com/%d7%97%d7%93%d7%a9%d7%95%d7%aa-%d7%90%d7%a9%d7%93%d7%95%d7%93/43396)
- **Alon Tavor dairy effluent:** ~4,000 m3/day, treated in an SBR operated by Palgey Maim (99.5% BOD removal). [environmentindex](https://www.environmentindex.com/en/article/new-research-finds-a-method-to-make-the-biological-treatment-of-industrial-wastewater-more-efficient-54.aspx)
- **National context:** Israel cow-milk production was 1.560 bn L in 2024. [Israel Dairy Board yearbook 2024](https://www.halavi.org.il/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/%D7%A9%D7%A0%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%9F-2024.pdf) The DB row FOODAG-0021 gives Israel cheese whey at ~810 kt/yr.

**ESG and targets:**
- No standalone Tnuva ESG report with quantified waste/water targets was found. Maala's rating covers energy, water, waste and goals. [Maala](https://maala.org.il/en/ranking/tnuva-25/)
- The 2023 strategy (via Calcalist/CTech) targets net-zero emissions by 2050, solar at 10 production sites, and recyclable laminate cheese lids (~186 t/yr of plastic avoided). [CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/hksueidl6)
- First electric truck in Israel. [walla](https://finance.walla.co.il/item/3671406)

**Innovation channels:**
- **Tnuva Ventures** and the **Fresh Start** foodtech incubator (Tnuva + Tempo + OurCrowd + Finistere). Stated interests include waste reduction and material reuse, and packaging that cuts waste and carbon. The portfolio includes MadeRight (mycelium packaging grown on industrial organic waste) and BountiCa. [Fresh Start](https://fresh-start.co.il/fresh-ideas/), [foodnavigator](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2023/08/24/maderight-mycelia-based-packaging-pioneer-mushrooms-with-investment/)
- Earlier "Capsule" accelerator. [CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3758143,00.html)
- Investor in Remilk (precision fermentation). [ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/israeli-food-tech-startup-remilk-raises-120m-investment-for-cow-free-milk/)
- Ba'emek works with **EXOSOMM** to extract milk exosomes from cheese whey. Treat exosome extraction as **not novel** for Tnuva. [Dairy Industries](https://www.dairyindustries.com/news/42468/exosomm-brings-new-bioactive-ingredient-to-medical-food-space/)

**Technion / Carasso link:** On 15 Apr 2026 Tnuva co-hosted the FoodTech session of the Technion Innovation Leadership Forum with the Carasso Center. [cftic](https://cftic.technion.ac.il/news/foodtech-innovation-in-the-industrial-accelerator-carasso-center-participates-in-the-technion-innovation-leadership-forum/)

**Likely challenges:**
1. COW water and delactosed permeate (low-value, high-volume end of the whey line).
2. Salty cheese brine / salt whey (the regulatory history makes this a sore point).
3. Acid whey and CIP sodium.

Meat by-products (blood, rumen) are a second tier.

## 2. Strauss Group (Platinum)

**Who:** Israeli food and beverage group. 2024 production was 658,230 t across all sites. Strauss Israel has 11 operational areas and 14 sites.

**Israeli sites:**
- Ahihud dairy (main dairy) and Yotvata dairy (kibbutz partnership).
- Elite confectionery, two plants at Nof HaGalil.
- Salty-snacks plant at Sderot (Strauss–Frito-Lay: Tapuchips, Doritos, Shush).
- Salads plant at Karmiel (Achla hummus).
- Elite coffee at Tzfat: roasting/packing only. Instant-coffee powder moved abroad in 2013. [Yeshiva World](https://www.theyeshivaworld.com/news/headlines-breaking-stories/201818/strauss-shutting-the-elite-coffee-factory-in-tzfas.html), [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.co.il/business/real-estate/article/2699859)
- Yad Mordechai honey, Kfar Masarik logistics centre, Strauss Water (Tami4).
- Sabra (US hummus) is outside Israel.

Sources: [ESG 2024](https://www.strauss-group.com/wp-content/blogs.dir/3/files/sites/3/121224_ESG_report_24_Eng-7Aug25.pdf), [Q3-2025 report](https://ir.strauss-group.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Reporting-package-ENG-Q3-2025.pdf).

**Published 2024 numbers (ESG report 2024, Sabra/Obela excluded):**

| Metric | 2024 value |
|---|---|
| Energy | 1.98 M GJ |
| Biogas used | 11.45 GWh, from a wastewater-to-biogas system |
| Scope 1 | 79,376 tCO2e (−16%) |
| Scope 2 (location-based) | 60,860 tCO2e |
| Intensity | 0.213 tCO2e/t product |
| Wastewater | 1,194,274 m3 |
| Water consumption | 565,943 m3 |
| Non-hazardous waste | 38,873 t total: 3,883 t landfilled, 4,253 t incinerated with energy recovery, 9,465 t recycled, 21,252 t reused |

The waste figures **exclude whey from Ahihud dairy, "most of which is used for feeding in dairy farms"**.

**Wastewater:**
- Strauss calls **sodium from CIP "a significant challenge"** for complying with effluent-reuse rules.
- 2024 fixes: NaOH replaced with potash-based cleaner at the confectionery plant; new CIP tank farm at the salads plant.
- ~80% of manufacturing water in Israel is recycled for irrigation.

**Targets for 2030:**
- 100% renewable electricity at Israeli sites.
- Zero industrial waste to landfill at Israeli sites.
- Halve food waste and landfill waste.
- Reusable, recyclable or degradable packaging (85% of purchased packaging was recyclable in 2024).

**Already done (so not novel):**
- Spent-coffee-ground boilers: Germany 2024; Tzfat 2009, a 6.8 MW system burning roasting residues *(snippet)*.
- Bottle crusher at Yotvata.
- Compactors at Ahihud and Sderot.
- Honey-return reuse.
- Water-bar refurbishment (15,000 units in 2024).

**Innovation channels:**
- **The Kitchen FoodTech Hub**, Strauss's incubator. Its portfolio includes **Yeap**, which upcycles spent yeast into functional protein, and Kokomodo (cell-cultured cacao). [The Kitchen](https://www.thekitchenhub.com/), [AgFunder](https://agfundernews.com/yeap-secures-investment-from-lesaffre-to-upcycle-yeast-into-a-functional-protein)
- Collaborates with Imagindairy (precision-fermentation whey). [AgFunder](https://agfundernews.com/imagindairy-acquires-industrial-scale-precision-fermentation-lines-for-animal-free-whey-this-will-allow-us-to-support-mass-market-adoption)

**Technion link:** Prof. Yoav Livney (Technion BFE) lists milk-protein research with Strauss on his CV *(snippet)*. [CV](https://senatesecretariat.net.technion.ac.il/files/2024/11/Yoav-D.-Livney-CV-8.9.24.pdf) No Strauss–Carasso side-stream project was found.

**Likely challenges:**
1. Sodium/salinity in CIP effluent.
2. Ahihud whey and returned dairy products (currently low-value feed).
3. Sderot snack fines, potato streams and frying oil (zero-landfill target).

Also possible: hummus aquafaba, multilayer snack packaging, Tami4 filter cartridges (SPONSOR-0009).

## 3. Magnum: The Magnum Ice Cream Company (TMICC), Israel (Gold)

**Identity (verified):**
- The logo hosted on sidestream2026.com reads "THE MAGNUM ICE CREAM COMPANY" with the Wall's/Heart logo.
- TMICC is Unilever's former ice-cream division. It demerged and listed on 8 Dec 2025 (Amsterdam/London/New York); Unilever keeps 19.9%.
- In Israel it is the former **Strauss Ice Cream** business, owned by Unilever since 1995–2014 and transferred to TMICC in 2025. CEO: Doron Zilberstein.
- On 28 May 2026 the company started replacing the "Strauss" trademark with **Magnum Co.**, finishing by end-2027. Brands: Magnum, Cremissimo, Cornetto, Solero, Twister, Crembo.
- **Single Israeli factory: Acre (Akko)**, an ice plant converted to ice cream in 1962.
- It is **not** Strauss Group: Strauss only receives trademark royalties until the rebrand ends.

Sources: [ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/sjcj100sxfg), [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/shopping/article/h1nz8lqclg), [LinkedIn – Zilberstein](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/doron-zilberstein-76114456_magnumco-tmicc-newchapter-activity-7465817743687307267-tIb4), [babli](https://www.babli.co.il/consumer-news/magnum-israel-rebranding-strauss-logo-change-1).

**Sustainability:**
- Net-zero ambition for 2050. No formal targets in 2025; formal targets, including packaging and cabinets, start being set in 2026.
- Factories already run on >60% renewable electricity.
- Main emission sources: factories, freezer-cabinet energy (a fleet of ~3 M cabinets), cocoa and dairy.
- First ice-cream brand to use recycled PP; plant-based Ben & Jerry's tubs in the EU.

Sources: [TMICC sustainability](https://corporate.magnumicecream.com/en/sustainability.html), [20-F 2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2071668/000110465926029805/micc-20251231x20f.htm).

**Acre site facts:**
- Large ammonia refrigeration plant. A 2019 leak led to enforcement by the Ministry of Environmental Protection (~17 t NH3 on site *(snippet)*). [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/environment-ministry-upbraids-ice-cream-factory-after-acre-ammonia-leak/)
- Wastewater of ~1,000 m3/day, compared with 16,000 m3/day capacity at the Acre WWTP (JPost 2011 *(snippet)*).
- Temporary shutdowns during the war to reduce ammonia storage. [ynetnews](https://www.ynetnews.com/business/article/rk30h9ota)

No Israel-specific innovation calls, startup programmes or Technion/Carasso collaborations were found for Magnum Israel.

**Likely challenges (up to 2):**
1. Changeover/rinse water (FOODAG-0162).
2. Off-spec ice cream and coating rework (SPONSOR-0003).

Also possible: refrigeration waste heat or cold-chain energy (SPONSOR-0004), freezer cabinets (SPONSOR-0005), multilayer wrappers.

## Cross-sponsor symbiosis ideas (for scoring later)

- **CBC Ashkelon fermentation CO2 → carbonation** at Bnei Brak/Neviot; or as a foaming agent in Tosaf foamed compounds.
- **CBC spent yeast → Strauss Kitchen portfolio (Yeap).**
- **Food side streams → Tosaf bio-fillers:** cocoa shells, BSG, sesame hulls, eggshell-like CaCO3 from whey minerals.
- **NVIDIA data-centre warm water (45–60 °C)** for low-temperature drying or membrane distillation of dairy and brewery streams in the Jezreel/Carmel cluster. Tnuva Alon Tavor, Tosaf Alon Tavor/Tnuvot/Gilboa and NVIDIA Yokneam/Mevo Carmel are all in the north.
